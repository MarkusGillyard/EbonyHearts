Run:   ./MortalSlopbat.sh
       (needs a Vulkan or OpenGL 3 capable graphics driver; tested on Linux Mint 22.3 x86_64)
       Add it to your application menu: ./install-desktop-entry.sh
       Force OpenGL: ./MortalSlopbat.sh --rendering-driver opengl3

Settings and progress are kept in ~/.local/share/MortalSlopbat/

CONTROLS (all keys can be changed in Settings)

                    Player 1     Player 2      Gamepad
  Move/jump/crouch  W A S D      Arrows        D-pad / left stick
  High Punch        U            Numpad 7      Y
  Block             I            Numpad 8      RB / RT
  High Kick         O            Numpad 9      B
  Low Punch         J            Numpad 4      X
  Run               K            Numpad 5      LB / LT
  Low Kick          L            Numpad 6      A
  Pause             Esc                        Start
  Full screen       F11 or Alt+Enter

In one-player modes both keyboard halves and any gamepad control Player 1.
In Versus, the Controllers screen decides who uses what.

FIGHTING
  Block is a button: stand to block highs and mids, crouch to block lows.
  Hold Run with forward to run; running and dial-a-combos spend the run meter.
  Down + High Punch: uppercut.  Back + Low Kick: sweep.  Back + High Kick: roundhouse.
  Forward or Back + Low Punch up close: throw (press a punch right after being grabbed to break).
  Every fighter's specials, combos and Sloptality: Pause -> Move List.
  When FINISH appears, stand at the right distance and enter your Sloptality.
