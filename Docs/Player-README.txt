MORTAL SLOPBAT
A fighting game by Markus Gillyard. Every river in the world ends in the Sump.

RUNNING IT
  Linux x86_64 with a Vulkan graphics driver (tested on an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2060).
  Unpack the archive anywhere and run:   ./MortalSlopbat.sh
  Nothing is installed; delete the folder to remove the game.

SAVES AND SETTINGS
  ~/.local/share/MortalSlopbat/UE5/settings.json and progress.json
  (under $XDG_DATA_HOME instead, when that is set).
  If you played the earlier Godot version, its progress is read once on first launch and
  carried over; its own files are never changed.

CONTROLS
  Controllers: PlayStation and Xbox pads. D-pad or left stick to move. Triangle/Y high
  punch, Square/X low punch, Circle/B high kick, Cross/A low kick; R1/RB or R2/RT block,
  L1/LB or L2/LT run. Buttons can be changed in Settings > Controls.
  Options/Start pauses; Cross/A confirms in menus, Circle/B goes back.
  Keyboard, left half:  W A S D move,  U J O L  high punch, low punch, high kick, low kick,
                        I block, K run.
  Keyboard, right half: arrow keys move, numeric keypad 7 4 9 6 high punch, low punch,
                        high kick, low kick, 8 block, 5 run.
  Esc pauses in a fight and goes back in menus; each keyboard half's Block also goes back.
  Training, Tutorial and Trials: R3/RS or Tab changes the dummy, skips a step or moves to the
  next trial; Create/View or F4 resets. In Training, F1 shows the boxes, F2 frame data.
  Two players can share one keyboard, use two pads, or one of each; Versus asks which device
  belongs to whom.

MODES
  Arcade (three towers, a rival, the Reflection, the Slopbat), Survival, Versus, Training
  (dummy settings, frame data, hitboxes), Tutorial, Trials (every fighter's combos), Extras
  (endings, records, move lists, credits) and Settings.
