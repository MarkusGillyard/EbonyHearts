EBONY HEARTS
A fighting game by Markus Gillyard. Every river in the world ends in the Sump.

RUNNING IT
  Linux x86_64 with a Vulkan graphics driver (tested on an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2060).
  Unpack the archive anywhere and run:   ./EbonyHearts.sh
  Nothing is installed; delete the folder to remove the game.

SAVES AND SETTINGS
  ~/.local/share/MortalSlopbat/UE5/settings.json and progress.json
  (under $XDG_DATA_HOME instead, when that is set; the folder's name is internal and has not
  changed, so saves from every earlier version carry on).
  If you played the earlier Godot version, its progress is read once on first launch and
  carried over; its own files are never changed.

TV, SPEAKERS AND SURROUND
  Settings > Video > Screen puts the game on another connected display, such as a TV, and it
  goes back there by itself next time. If a TV crops the edges of the picture, raise Screen
  margin on the same page.
  Sound goes to the output chosen in your system's sound settings: if the TV, soundbar or
  amplifier stays silent, choose it there. Settings > Sound > Speakers on Automatic gives a
  stereo mix on two channels (TV speakers, headphones, most soundbars) and a surround mix on
  four or more. For surround, set the system's output itself to 5.1 or 7.1 (for HDMI, its
  "Digital Surround" profile) and use a receiver or soundbar that takes multichannel PCM from
  the computer. A TV's own speakers are stereo, and many TVs pass on only stereo PCM.

CONTROLS
  Controllers: PlayStation and Xbox pads. D-pad or left stick to move. Triangle/Y high
  punch, Square/X low punch, Circle/B high kick, Cross/A low kick; R1/RB or R2/RT block,
  L1/LB or L2/LT run. Buttons can be changed in Settings > Controls.
  Options/Start pauses; Cross/A confirms in menus, Circle/B goes back.
  The game shows a connected controller's own buttons. Settings > Controls > Button prompts
  chooses Automatic, PlayStation, Xbox or PC (keyboard) instead.
  Keyboard, left half:  W A S D move,  U J O L  high punch, low punch, high kick, low kick,
                        I block, K run.
  Keyboard, right half: arrow keys move, numeric keypad 7 4 9 6 high punch, low punch,
                        high kick, low kick, 8 block, 5 run.
  Esc pauses in a fight and goes back in menus; each keyboard half's Block also goes back.
  Training, Tutorial and Trials: R3/RS or Tab changes the dummy, skips a step or moves to the
  next trial; Share/Create/View or F4 resets. In Training, F1 shows the boxes, F2 frame data.
  Two players can share one keyboard, use two pads, or one of each; Versus asks which device
  belongs to whom.

MODES
  Story (Wren's way through the tournament), Arcade (three towers, a rival, the Reflection, Hollis
  Fathom, the Ebony Heart Fragment), Survival, Versus, Training
  (dummy settings, frame data, hitboxes), Tutorial, Trials (every fighter's combos), Extras
  (endings, records, move lists, credits) and Settings.
