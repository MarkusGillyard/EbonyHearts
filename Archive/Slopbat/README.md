# The Slopbat

The game's original final boss, retired on 30 October 2026 when the Ebony Heart Fragment took its
own form. Kept whole, nothing deleted: `Data/fighter.json` (moves as they stood), `Data/poses/`,
`Data/looks/`, `Body/slopbat.fbx` (the winged body with half joints) and `portrait.png`. The body can
be rebuilt from `Tools/blender/fighters.json` (entry `slopbat`, outfit `slopbat`, in
`fighter_outfits2.py`) by setting its status back to `build`; its textures come with that build.
